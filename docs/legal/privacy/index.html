1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed through the Service. It does not apply to third-party services governed by their own privacy policies, including Apple, Google, event websites, or links shared by users.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and authentication information: email address, authentication-provider identifiers, and account identifiers. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive information that provider makes available based on your settings, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and a provider-specific identifier. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your password in Wimya’s profile database.
  • Profile information: name, title, biography, profile photo, and your Wimya account number or identifier.
  • Connections: pal requests, participants, status, and related timestamps.
  • Event information you choose to share: event name, web link, location, start and end times, source type, and technical identifiers used to recognize duplicates.
  • Communications: information you include when contacting us for support, privacy requests, or other inquiries.

Information collected automatically

Our infrastructure and service providers may automatically receive limited technical and usage information needed to deliver, secure, and maintain the Service, such as IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, language, time zone, request timestamps, authentication events, diagnostics, and security or fraud signals. Firebase Remote Config may use an app-instance identifier and device information to deliver configuration values.

Wimya does not currently use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution, or marketing SDKs. We may adopt such tools later. Before doing so, we will update this Policy and provide any notice, choice, or consent required by law.

3. Calendar, camera, and photo access

  • Calendar: with your permission, Wimya reads calendar events on your device so you can review and select events to share. We upload only events you affirmatively select. For selected events, we may store the event details listed above and calendar and event identifiers used to prevent duplicate imports. Those source identifiers are restricted to your account.
  • Camera: with your permission, Wimya uses the camera to scan QR codes for connecting with pals. Camera images used for live QR scanning are processed for that purpose and are not uploaded by Wimya.
  • Photos: when you choose a profile photo, the processed image is uploaded and stored as part of your profile. If you choose an image to scan a QR code, Wimya accesses only the image you select and processes it on your device for that purpose.

You can change device permissions in iOS Settings, although disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create, authenticate, and maintain accounts;
  • provide profiles, pal connections, event sharing, feeds, and related features;
  • show shared events to the account owner and accepted pals;
  • process selected profile photos and prevent duplicate event imports;
  • configure, operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests;
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms; and
  • comply with law and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users, Snaap, and others.

5. How we disclose information

We may disclose personal information in the following circumstances:

  • To other Wimya users: signed-in users may see profile information. Accepted pals may see the events you share, including event names, links, locations, and times. Pal-request information is available to the users involved.
  • To service providers: vendors process information for us to provide hosting, authentication, database, storage, configuration, security, and technical operations. Current providers include Google’s Firebase services. Apple and Google process information when you use their sign-in services.
  • For legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, security, investigate misuse, or enforce agreements.
  • In a business transaction: information may be disclosed in connection with financing, due diligence, a merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.
  • With your direction or consent: we may disclose information when you direct us to do so or otherwise consent.

No sale or behavioral advertising. We do not currently sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal information to serve targeted advertising. If these practices change, we will update this Policy and provide legally required rights, notices, and choices before or when the change takes effect.

6. Visibility and your choices

  • Profiles: your name, title, biography, profile photo, and account identifier may be visible to other signed-in Wimya users.
  • Events: shared events are visible to you and your accepted pals. Event-source identifiers used for importing are visible only to you and our service providers.
  • Connections: pal requests and relationship status are visible to the users involved.
  • Permissions: you may manage calendar, camera, and photo access through your device and in-app choices.
  • Authentication: you may manage certain sign-in information through Apple, Google, or your applicable provider.

Be thoughtful about sharing event locations, meeting links, and information relating to other people. Information another user has already viewed or copied may remain outside our control.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the Service, maintain your account, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of information, why we collected it, operational needs, sensitivity, and legal requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it. Some information may remain for a limited period in backups or where retention is necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or establishing or defending legal claims.

8. Data security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including authenticated access controls and restrictions based on account relationships. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, lost, or altered without authorization. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and notifying us if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and to appeal our response. You may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

To delete your account or submit a privacy request, email runtimesquad@gmail.com. Describe your request and the email address associated with your Wimya account. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.
